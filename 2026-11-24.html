Lawmakers in both parties objected, Elizabeth Warren saying Nvidia still owes Congress answers, and House Speaker-designate Hakeem Jeffries promised a wide-ranging investigation of Nvidia and Lutnick once Democrats take over in January. Governance experts quoted by Al Jazeera called the public presidential pressure unprecedented and warned it could invite heavier scrutiny later. Huang holds only about 3.6% of the company.

SCOTUSblog

The Supreme Court hears Viramontes v.

The Supreme Court hears Viramontes v. Cook County, a challenge to state and local AR-15 bans, on December 2. In a SCOTUSblog outside-opinion piece, Georgia State law professor Andrew Willinger says the debate set off by a New York Times report, that Justice Scalia's "dangerous and unusual" wording in Heller misquoted Blackstone's "dangerous or unusual", misses the main problem. Willinger's point is that the phrase was dicta, never tested in litigation, which Justice Alito's 2016 Caetano concurrence recast as a two-part test that lower courts now apply through ownership and sales data. The 3rd Circuit read it to protect widely owned AR-15s, while a Massachusetts judge upheld that state's ban. He expects most justices to treat the standard as settled anyway, and argues that relying on stray dicta runs against Scalia's own approach.

The UN climate summit in Antalya broke up on Sunday after the EU and other countries rejected a final text that dropped any commitment to transition away from fossil fuels.
