FT Inside Politics

The FT's Whitehall editor argues that after months of little attention from ministers and MPs, the government, the intelligence services and parliament have shifted into gear this week.

The FT's Whitehall editor argues that after months of little attention from ministers and MPs, the government, the intelligence services and parliament have shifted into gear this week. AI minister Kanishka Narayan said the technology must face regulation and set out plans for compulsory safety reporting and monitoring of frontier AI, plus a possible requirement to make models available for pre-release testing. He warned that a breach of Britain's cyber defences by rogue agents is "likely, if not inevitable". MI5's director-general devoted much of his annual threat update to AI, describing it as supercharging the attacker-defender cyber contest and announcing a new AI Threat Assessment Centre that pools MI5, the AI Security Institute, the National Crime Agency and the NCSC. The Commons business and trade committee opened a 12-month inquiry with a three-hour hearing. The piece also cites Anthropic's warning about AI worms hijacking smart-home devices and a cyber attack that raised temperatures in French supermarket fridges, and notes the earlier withholding of a model from the AI Security Institute was reversed. It is an opinionated newsletter column, so the characterisations are the author's.

Markets and credit: 23-hour trading and Drahi's creditors

FT Unhedged

Unhedged says the SEC-led push for "24-hour" trading is expected to start on 6 December across five exchanges including Nasdaq and NYSE Arca, but it is really 23 hours a day, five days a week, with a one-hour pause from 8pm to 9pm EST Monday to Thursday.

Unhedged says the SEC-led push for "24-hour" trading is expected to start on 6 December across five exchanges including Nasdaq and NYSE Arca, but it is really 23 hours a day, five days a week, with a one-hour pause from 8pm to 9pm EST Monday to Thursday. The authors attribute part of the pressure to crypto and tokenisation and to demand from overseas retail traders, and note that overnight volume is up 358% year on year but is only about 0.1% of regular-hours volume, mostly in sub-$1 stocks. They worry about thin liquidity and manipulation risk, a lack of SEC guidance on overnight risk management, and regulatory staffing, and doubt institutions will join soon. The same issue says the big US banks' latest results were healthy: Goldman's equities trading revenue rose 72% to $6.4bn and JPMorgan's nearly 50% to $5bn, while fixed income, currency and commodities trading fell on bond-market turbulence.

FT Due Diligence

Due Diligence reports that creditors to Altice International, including Elliott Management, Sona Asset Management and Arini Capital Management, sued the company and linked entities on Tuesday.

Due Diligence reports that creditors to Altice International, including Elliott Management, Sona Asset Management and Arini Capital Management, sued the company and linked entities on Tuesday. They allege assets worth billions were moved beyond their reach and say they are owed €8bn. It follows a similar suit two weeks ago by lenders to the US business, including Apollo and Oaktree, and a separate Altice suit calling the US bondholders an "illegal cartel", after which Kirkland & Ellis quit as the group's counsel. The newsletter adds that Drahi agreed a €20.35bn sale of SFR in June after Altice France's debt was cut from €24bn to €15.5bn, and that LSEG confirmed talks to buy credit-data provider Octus for $3.5bn, with LSEG shares down nearly 5%.
