OpenAI says it has built industry-leading protections for young users and wants rules that apply to the whole industry rather than one company. The suits come as OpenAI prepares to go public.

Bitcoin: quantum risk and a steadier price

CoinDesk

Nine crypto companies, including Hacken, the Midnight Foundation, Quantus and Postquant Labs, launched a Quantum Alliance to write shared security tests and migration guidance for quantum-resistant blockchains.

Nine crypto companies, including Hacken, the Midnight Foundation, Quantus and Postquant Labs, launched a Quantum Alliance to write shared security tests and migration guidance for quantum-resistant blockchains. It estimates that 6.4 million BTC, 30.2% of supply, sit at addresses whose public keys are already visible and could one day be cracked, although no existing machine can do it. Experts disagree on timing: Postquant's Richard Carback sees 2028 as realistic, while The Vault's Bryn Bennett says nobody knows and the real question is whether custodians can migrate in time. They also split on what to do with vulnerable coins such as Satoshi's roughly 1.1 million. Carback prefers moving them or holding them in a trust that owners can claim, while Bennett warns that freezing coins would set a precedent on property rights that only the Bitcoin network itself can decide. Changing the signatures would need agreement across developers, miners, wallets and exchanges and could take up more block space.
