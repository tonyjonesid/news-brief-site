Anthropic sold 980 million shares at $83 each, raising $81.3bn and overtaking the $75bn SpaceX raised at its June debut (SpaceX's total later reached $85.7bn once underwriters bought extra shares). Demand ran far ahead of the plan: last week's filings proposed 825 million shares at $70 to $78, and Al Jazeera says investors reportedly asked for six times the shares on offer. A separate $26bn private placement takes the total raised to $107.3bn. The price values the Claude maker at roughly $1.5tn, which would make it the world's 12th-largest company, just behind Tesla, and about 55% above the $965bn valuation of its $65bn private round in May. Trading starts on Thursday on the Nasdaq under the ticker "ANT". The prospectus shows $33.2bn of revenue in the first nine months of 2026, $17bn of it in the third quarter, alongside a $104bn net loss for the period that it attributes mostly to non-cash accounting items, and nearly $901bn of computing and infrastructure commitments. The listing is being treated as a test of AI valuations after a month in which the Nasdaq Composite fell 7.6% and Nvidia's shares dropped more than 30%. Analyst Dan Ives called it a near-term win that should restore some confidence in the sector. WSJ's What's News adds that the company sold more shares than planned at a higher price than expected.

The Guardian

The International Energy Agency's annual report expects datacentres to use twice as much electricity by 2030 as they do now.

The International Energy Agency's annual report expects datacentres to use twice as much electricity by 2030 as they do now. AI accounts for three-quarters of that growth, adding about 350TWh a year, which is more than Great Britain uses. The US and China are leaning on fossil fuels to meet the demand. The agency expects coal to supply about half the power for China's AI datacentres and gas about 60% in the US, where around 180GW of datacentre-linked gas plants were planned as of August, almost the EU's entire installed gas capacity. In the EU, by contrast, renewables should cover about 70% of datacentre power. The agency estimates that datacentre capacity has grown tenfold in two years to 17GW, based on AI-server shipments and satellite tracking of 93 large "AI factories". Fatih Birol said the energy system will not return to how it was before the Middle East war, which the IEA calls the biggest energy-market disruption in history.

On the sidelines of the APEC summit in Shenzhen, Donald Trump, Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin met for about 55 minutes behind closed doors, after a joint photo. On Tuesday a planned missile-and-drone attack brought down a 227-metre span of the road bridge between Russia and occupied Crimea.
